I've been fitting hearing aids for a while and I keep coming back to one thing.
The presence of non-personal text by itself suppresses what you can read out of a
comment [[mech:dilution_not_length]]. I ran into this again last week with a
thread that was mostly quoted spec sheets. My own take is that you can't fix it by
trimming length. You have to look at what share of the text is the person talking.
That's what I'd check first. It took me a while to see it.
