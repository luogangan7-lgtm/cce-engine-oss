Try this before your next call to a government line: turn LE Audio off on the phone, and if there is a call audio codec setting, pin it to a fixed option instead of adaptive. On the Exynos S25 FE that pair is what usually stops this. It costs you nothing and you will know on the next call.

Why it stops. Your aids carry your voice, so calls are running Classic Bluetooth HFP, not ASHA. ASHA has no microphone path at all, which is the answer to which of the four you are on. Switchboard calls hand you between IVR, hold music and a live agent, and every handoff renegotiates the codec. Each renegotiation tears the voice link down and rebuilds it. That is your three to four seconds of clean silence with no crackle. Nothing is degrading. The link is dropping and coming back. Pin the codec and there is nothing left to renegotiate.

If it still cycles after that, one call settles it. Ring the same number with Bluetooth off, on speaker. Still cycling means the fault is in the network leg and no hearing aid setting will touch it, which at least stops you buying anything. Clean means we are back on the link and I will keep going with you until it is fixed.

One boundary. I cannot confirm the Phonak build from outside, and that opacity is deliberate on the brand side, so do not spend effort there. None of this depends on who made them.
